VPN Selectivo

The apps you choose go through Mullvad; the rest of your Mac uses your normal connection. If the VPN fails, protected apps lose Internet access: they never go out without the VPN.

Daily use

  • Open VPN Selectivo from the Dock: VPN switch, live status (server and exit IP), location (Ubicación), Auto-VPN and the list of apps to check (through Mullvad) or uncheck (normal connection). No password needed.
  • Auto-VPN (⚡): opening an app with ⚡ turns the VPN on; it turns off 2 minutes after they are all closed. Checked apps without ⚡ only have Internet access while the VPN is on.
  • You can also open apps with their "‹App› VPN" launchers (folder /Applications/VPN Selectivo; drag them to the Dock) or from the menu-bar shield.
  • If you open a protected app with its original icon, it is closed instantly and reopened protected (Chromium browsers restore their tabs).
  • Apps that are not protected are never touched.

The menu-bar shield

Shield with lockProtected. Protected apps go through Mullvad.
Half shieldThe server is not responding: protected traffic is stopped (it does not go out without the VPN).
Shield with ✕Protected apps are open but have NO Internet access (connecting, or you switched the VPN off).
Empty shieldIdle. It connects when you open an app with ⚡ or turn the switch on.
Shield with !The Mullvad configuration is missing, or the firewall was altered (protected apps are frozen until it is repaired).
TriangleThe service is not responding. Check the log or reinstall.

Buttons (the interface is in Spanish)

  • Comprobar IP (check IP): compares the exit IP of protected apps (Mullvad) with your direct IP.
  • Autodiagnóstico (self-test): PF, tunnel, IPs, IPv6, UDP and a deliberate tunnel drop (about 30 s).
  • Vigilar fugas (watch for leaks): with protected apps open, looks for their packets (or packets sent by system services on their behalf) outside the tunnel.
  • Importar configuración (import configuration): a .conf or .zip from mullvad.net › WireGuard configuration (asks for the administrator password).
  • Registro (log): opens /Library/Logs/VPNSelectivo.log.
  • When you check or uncheck an app that is running: Reiniciar ahora (restart now: quits cleanly and reopens it in/out of the tunnel) or Al cerrarla (when I close it).

Important

  • Don't use the official Mullvad app at the same time: while it is connected, your whole Mac goes through the VPN. Disconnect it; no need to uninstall it.
  • What cannot go through the tunnel: work done by system services on behalf of an app (embedded web pages using WebKit, background downloads) and the hostnames looked up through the system DNS.
  • Casting to a TV (Chromecast/AirPlay) from a protected app does not work while the local network is blocked (ALLOW_LAN=0).
  • Log: /Library/Logs/VPNSelectivo.log. Uninstall: Uninstall.command in the downloaded folder.
